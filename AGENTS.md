# HVAC Smart Site Front End (template)

Read `README.md`, `REMIX.md`, and `RUNBOOK.md` before changing this repository. This is a reusable front end for HVAC smart sites. The checked-in brand ("Northline Heating & Air", the Tri-County area, the placeholder logo) is a demo and must be replaced per client through `REMIX.md`.

Preserve the dark premium direction and the primary **Book Service** CTA. Keep every visual literal to the trade: airflow, heat and cold, fans, equipment, local landscape. No abstract orbs or space imagery.

Do not invent testimonials, certifications, phone numbers, financing terms, response times, or emergency availability. Online requests stay pending until the office confirms an appointment. Keep credentials out of code, commits, and output. GrowthOS integrations require a real approved endpoint and contract before being described as live.

Do not put a CSS `transition` on `transform` for any element GSAP animates; they conflict and the animation freezes.
