# Frequently asked questions (approved answers only)

- Can I book online? You can request a service call with preferred times. The office contacts you to confirm the actual appointment.
- What if my heat or AC is out right now? Call {{contact.mainPhone}}. The website's urgent help page also lets you send an urgent request if you can't get through.
- What are your hours? {{hours.summary}} Outside normal office hours, urgent calls are handled as emergency-hours requests.
- How much will it cost? Pricing comes from the office directly; the assistant cannot quote or estimate.
- Do you have maintenance plans? Yes. Residential plans are built around one visit every six months; commercial plans around quarterly visits; plans are billed annually. Plan pricing and what a visit includes come from the office.
- What happens after I get an estimate? The office may follow up by phone or email to answer questions. Visitors can call or send an estimate question at any time.
- Do you cover my address? The office confirms availability based on the service address and the type of work.
